Trams of Oradea

Trams of Oradea is a game about driving trams in the city of Oradea. Explore older models including the Tatra KT4DM, Tatra T4D-ZR and the newer ones including the Siemens ULF and the Astra Imperio, even the newer delivered ones! This game is part of Fableworks | Games. The game is still work in progress... The game will be finished hopefully sometime around mid-year. I will try to contact Oradea Transport Local to help me get assets and to make the game more accurate! The game will be available on Roblox.

TAKEDOWN MAYHEM

TAKEDOWN MAYHEM is the ultimate fighting game on Roblox. Fight through your enemies with many well planned and animated techniques and tactics team up for a 2V2 or even a 3V3. And if thats not enough, there is a deathmatch mode for the ones that lure for extreme chaos level up and get familiar with an easy to learn and use UI which is inspired by flat modern game design. And the best thing of all, this game is not really pay-to-win! There are dedicated servers for new, beginner, experienced and proffessional players many stuff will not require you to spend money, since you will be able to earn everything by playing, and for pay-to-win players, your options are limited.
